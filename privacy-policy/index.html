Privacy Policy

Privacy policy for SemBeacon libraries and applications

SemBeacon Application

SemBeacon is an application maintained by MVdW-Software, the software consultancy of Dr. Maxim Van de Wynckel, for scanning and retrieving information about Bluetooth Low Energy SemBeacons and other related beacons such as iBeacon, AltBeacon or Eddystone. SemBeacon was created as part of Dr. Van de Wynckel's PhD research at the Vrije Universiteit Brussel.

The application will scan for nearby beacons, which requires the use of the location permission. This information is not shared with SemBeacon or other parties and is only used to process the beacon information. This collection is necessary for the functioning of the application whose only functionality is the scanning of beacons.

Solid Login

SemBeacon does not require users to log in, but offers the possibility to login via Solid. User accounts can be created with a Pod provider which can be chosen by the user or self hosted. Any account deletion and terms of service has to be checked with the Pod provider.

Pod Access

The application will retrieve the user information (WebID) to enable the user to broadcast its WebID using a simulated SemBeacon. The application will not store or access any other information in the Pod unless referenced in the WebID resource.

Permissions

  • Location (Precise): This permission is used both for getting the GPS location in the Beacon Map page as well as a required permission to scan for beacons as these beacons can be used to determine the location.
  • Nearby Devices: This permission allows nearby devices to detect your phone. This permission is used for the simulation of beacons.
  • Notifications: An "ongoing" notification is used when simulating a beacon. This is to ensure that users are aware that the phone is broadcasting a beacon in the background.

App and Device Integrity

On Android, the app may send an integrity token to SemBeacon's shortener service when it shortens a link. The token is generated by Google Play and contains information about the app installation (whether it was installed and signed by Google Play and whether it was modified) and the device (whether it passes Android's device integrity checks). It contains a hash of the request, not the link in readable form, and does not contain your name or account information. We use the verdict only to rate-limit abuse and never to identify you. Google processes this data as described in the Play Integrity API terms. The check is optional: on the web, in emulators, in sideloaded builds and during a Google outage the app continues to work without it.

Diagnostics

The application writes diagnostic logs to a local app folder on your device. These logs stay on the device and are not sent to SemBeacon or third parties; they are only shared if you choose to export them.

Changes

Changes to the privacy policy will be mentioned within app updates or as modification on this website.
