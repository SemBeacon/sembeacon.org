## About

*SemBeacon* is an Android and iOS application that implements the SemBeacon specification. It is primarily used as a demonstrator application of the specification. It supports scanning for iBeacon, AltBeacon, Eddystone* and SemBeacons.

The application will retrieve map information and visualise this information to the user. It will also fetch information of other non-SemBeacons that are detected. On Android, users can also simulate all different beacon types to test and design SemBeacon-compatible systems.

## Scanning

Bluetooth scanning can be started or stopped by pressing the *play* button at the bottom right of the screen. This button is available on the scanning page and map.
